Your clips become AI tools for kiting

We're building the open training dataset for kitesurfing — thousands of short, labelled trick clips shared by riders. It's what will let us build things like:

  • 🎬 Automatic clip cutting — drop in a long session video and get it sliced into individual tricks, each one named, plus editing tools built on top
  • 🔍 Trick recognition — models that can identify tricks and sort clips automatically
  • 🌍 A world model for kiting — generative video that actually understands wind, water, and tricks

None of that exists without real footage from real riders. Every clip you share makes the tools better for everyone — and the dataset stays open, for anyone to build on.
